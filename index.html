Core Philosophy

Human Amplification
over Full Automation

AI should enhance human expertise rather than replace it. In high-stakes environments where poor decisions can lead to explosions, environmental disasters, or expensive failures. Having a human-in-the-loop architecture is not a workaround, it's intrinsic part of Agentic architecture.

Although people should embrace an AI-native mentality, I challenge the "automate everything" mentality, especially for translating complex security data into the operational language of engineers who manage physical systems. A human-centered design approach is key to success.
